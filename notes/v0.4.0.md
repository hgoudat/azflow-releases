## What's new

- **Cloud for everyone.** Every account can run agents in the Cloud. The Free plan includes 5 Cloud hours a month, one thread at a time.
- **Billing & Plan in the app.** Change plans, and buy any amount of Azflow AI credit from $10 to $500.
- **Organizations.** Members and roles, team threads you can watch and fork, and Share live for local threads.
- **Azflow AI.** Hosted models with monthly credit on every plan; no AI subscription needed.
- **A new sign-in screen**, in light and dark.
- **Automatic updates.** From this version on, the app updates itself.
