## What's new

Team GPU: use the models your teammates share from their computers.

- **Team GPU for organizations.** An Owner or Admin turns it on in Settings → Team GPU. It is off until they do.
- **Share your models.** Share the models in LM Studio on your computer with your organization. You choose which models and how many requests run at a time, and you can pause.
- **Use shared models.** Teammates pick the Team GPU agent to use a shared model. The agent is listed while at least one model is shared.
- **Inside your organization only.** Requests run in LM Studio on the sharing computer, so its owner could read them.
- **PowerShell exit codes.** A terminal started with a command in PowerShell now reports the program's own exit code instead of 0 or 1.
